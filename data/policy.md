# Company Resolution Policies

Guidelines for resolving customer delivery issues.

---

## Refund Policy

Refund decisions are **binary** (full refund or no refund).

### When to Approve Refund
- Lost package (confirmed by carrier)
- Damaged goods (with photo evidence)
- Wrong item delivered
- Delivery delay **7+ days** past promised date
- Customer explicitly requests refund and has valid complaint

### When to Deny Refund
- Order delivered on time
- Delay less than 3 days with no other issues
- No valid complaint or evidence

### Escalation Required
- Customer rating **5 stars** (VIP) → Human review
- **More than 3** care calls on same order → Human review
- Order value **over $500** → Manager approval

---

## Reschedule Policy

### Allowed Timeframes
- Can reschedule up to **7 days** from original date
- Same-day reschedule only before **2:00 PM** local time
- No weekend deliveries available

### Fees
- No fee for company-fault delays
- No fee for first reschedule
- $5 fee for subsequent reschedules (waived for VIP)

---

## Escalation Policy

### Automatic Escalation Triggers
- Customer rating: **5 stars** (VIP treatment)
- Care calls: **More than 3** calls on same order
- Order value: **Over $500**
- Repeat complaints: **2 or more** on same issue

### Escalation Actions
- VIP customers → Priority human review
- High-value orders → Manager approval for resolution
- Repeat issues → Root cause investigation

---

## Communication Guidelines

### Response Times
- Initial response: Within **24 hours**
- Follow-up after resolution: Within **48 hours**

### Tone Requirements
- Always apologize for delays/issues
- Acknowledge customer frustration
- Offer clear resolution options
- Use "we" for company, personalize with order details

### Message Structure
1. Acknowledge the issue
2. Apologize sincerely
3. Explain what happened (briefly)
4. Offer resolution options
5. Confirm next steps
